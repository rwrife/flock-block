Flock Block is an original, offline iPhone arcade game about placing blockers instead of steering birds.

Four incoming lanes give you one job: read the flock and decide where to put your next obstacle. Pick from six blockers — a cardboard wall, trampoline, sticky patch, fan, balloon net and decoy feeder — then tap a lane to place it. Earn coins from waves, unlock more blockers without purchases, and decide whether to let bonus-tagged birds through for extra points. Replay a wave or move to the next one.

Your score, coins, unlocked blockers and haptic setting stay on your iPhone. Export a plain JSON save, import one with confirmation, or delete local data with confirmation. No account, cloud sync, ads, in-app purchases or network connection.

Designed for portrait iPhone with a blocker tray below the four-lane field. Large tap targets, labelled controls and lane status support VoiceOver; the tray scrolls with large text, and the field respects system Reduce Motion and Increased Contrast settings. Haptic feedback is optional and every action has visual and spoken status.

Flock Block uses original artwork and a playful, non-violent theme. No dual-screen mode or iPad version is included.
